# E-Commerce Customer Churn & Retention

## Business Problem

The company wants to identify active customers who are at risk of
churning early enough for the retention team to intervene.

## Objective

Predict customer churn risk and develop a profitable customer
retention strategy.

## Project Goal

The final objective is not only to build a predictive model,
but to determine:

- Which customers should be targeted?
- What retention action should be taken?
- How much could the company potentially save?
- Is the intervention profitable?

## Project Status

Currently in the Data Understanding stage.
